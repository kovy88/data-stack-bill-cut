6. Make 4 high-churn permanent tables transient

$1,106 – $1,266 per year, medium confidence. ANALYTICS.STAGING, SANDBOX.DS.

Recreate them as TRANSIENT in the pipeline that builds them (CREATE TRANSIENT TABLE …, dbt `transient: true`) or move them to a TRANSIENT schema. Fail-safe bytes disappear 7 days after the switch.

  • 4.01 TB in fail-safe and 0.58 TB in time travel on tables whose fail-safe is larger than their live data, a sign they are rebuilt or reloaded often.
  • Lower bound counts fail-safe only; upper bound also counts time travel (transient tables with DATA_RETENTION_TIME_IN_DAYS = 0).

Risks

  • Transient tables have no fail-safe: once time travel expires, Snowflake support cannot recover overwritten data. Use only for tables rebuildable from source.

Method

  • Source: SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE and SHOW WAREHOUSES, exported by you. No table data was read.
  • A warehouse is billed per second from the first query until AUTO_SUSPEND seconds after the last one, with a 60-second minimum per resume.
  • If the model overestimates the metered bill, the saving is scaled down. It is never scaled up.
  • Annual figures assume a similar workload. A downsizing range is computed after the auto-suspend change on the same warehouse.
  • Prices: 3.00 USD per credit and 23.00 USD per TB-month, as given by you.
  • Findings under $100 per year are left out.
