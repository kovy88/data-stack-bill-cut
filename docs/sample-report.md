- **Action:** Recreate them as TRANSIENT in the pipeline that builds them (CREATE TRANSIENT TABLE …, dbt `transient: true`) or move them to a TRANSIENT schema. Fail-safe bytes disappear 7 days after the switch.

Evidence:

- 4.01 TB in fail-safe and 0.58 TB in time travel on tables whose fail-safe is larger than their live data, a sign they are rebuilt or reloaded often.

Risks:

- Transient tables have no fail-safe: once time travel expires, Snowflake support cannot recover overwritten data. Use only for tables rebuildable from source.

## Method and assumptions

- Source: SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE (WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY, QUERY_HISTORY, TABLE_STORAGE_METRICS) and SHOW WAREHOUSES, exported by you. No table data was read.
- Billing model: a warehouse is billed per second from the first query until AUTO_SUSPEND seconds after the last one, with a 60-second minimum per resume. Busy time comes from query execution intervals (after compilation and queueing).
- The model is checked against metered credits per warehouse. If it overestimates the bill, savings are scaled down to match; they are never scaled up.
- Annual figures = saving in the period × 365 / period length; they assume a similar workload. Savings add up: the downsizing range is computed after the auto-suspend change on the same warehouse.
- Prices: 3.00 USD per credit and 23.00 USD per TB-month (1 TB = 1024⁴ bytes), as given by you. Cloud services, serverless features and data transfer are not included.
- Findings under $100 per year are left out.
